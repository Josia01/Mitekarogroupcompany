Flagship businesses

Leveraging technology to connect farmers to markets & move goods efficiently

Vuna Marketplace

www.vunamarketplace.org

Where farmers & consumers meet directly — Vuna is an agri-tech platform that eliminates middlemen. Farmers list fresh produce, consumers and local businesses buy at fair prices.

  • Real‑time market prices & weather insights
  • Mobile‑first platform for rural farmers
  • Secure payments and quality assurance
  • Reduce post‑harvest loss by connecting directly to buyers
Visit Vuna Marketplace

Ashoko Logistics

www.ashoko.com

Reliable transport & shipping across the region — Ashoko moves goods efficiently, from fresh farm produce to industrial equipment. Real‑time tracking and a modern fleet.

  • Nationwide coverage & cross‑border shipping
  • Cold chain for perishables (integrated with Vuna)
  • AI‑optimised routes to reduce fuel & time
  • Warehousing & last‑mile delivery
Visit Ashoko Logistics

Vuna + Ashoko : full‑circle solution

Farmers using Vuna Marketplace can instantly book Ashoko logistics to deliver their produce to urban buyers. This integration creates a seamless farm‑to‑fork chain — technology and transport united.
