How cargo moves

By road across Africa. By sea to the world.

Grain comes off the fields in Tunduma, Shinyanga, and Mbeya. Timber comes off our plantation in Iringa. Everything meets at head office in Dar es Salaam — some of it stays on the truck for regional delivery, the rest goes to port.

Channel 01

Road freight

MGL trucks haul grain, beans, and timber overland — both within Tanzania and across neighbouring borders — for buyers who need cargo delivered directly, not shipped.

ZambiaMalawiDR CongoKenyaRegional Tanzania
Channel 02

Sea freight

Cargo bound for overseas buyers is consolidated at our Dar es Salaam head office and shipped out through the Port of Dar es Salaam.
